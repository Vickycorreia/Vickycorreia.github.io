01The objective

Develop a prototype device to test an aircraft cargo Power Drive Unit. The project centres on providing a practical interface for routine checks in an aerospace maintenance context.

02The approach

The device combines an Arduino UNO R4, CAN communication and an LCD interface. These components bring embedded electronics and the equipment’s communication link together in one prototype.

03My contribution

During my internship, I developed the prototype device. The work connected my interests in electronics, embedded systems and aerospace through a concrete testing application.
