01The objective

Explore stereo audio transmission over an infrared link. The project brings together the analog circuits needed to transmit the signal and recover it at the receiver.

02The approach

Our current work focuses on transmitter and receiver circuits, FM modulation and signal recovery. Infrared communication, analog electronics and FM / PLL techniques are the main technical areas of the project.

03My contribution

I am working on the transmitter and receiver circuits, FM modulation and signal recovery. This work is still in progress as part of our ongoing electronics project.
