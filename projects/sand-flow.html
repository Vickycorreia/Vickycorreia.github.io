The three main strands of the experimental study.

01The objective

Study the behaviour of granular sand through experimentation. The project connects physical measurements with mathematical modelling to build an understanding of sand flow.

02The approach

The study brings together experimentation, modelling and analysis. Measurements provide the connection to the physical behaviour of the sand, while mathematical modelling supports the interpretation of the findings.

03My contribution

My work on this study connects experimental measurements with mathematical modelling and the clear presentation of the findings. It reflects my interest in understanding how a system behaves through observation and analysis.
